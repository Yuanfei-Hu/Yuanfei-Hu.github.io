Yuanfei (Cheyenne) Hu

Computational neuroscience · applied mathematics · machine learning

I am interested in how memory and adaptive behavior emerge from neural dynamics. My work uses mathematical models, simulation, and machine learning to study persistent activity, replay, and structured representations.

I am currently completing an MSc in Biomedical Informatics at the National University of Singapore. Before this, I studied applied mathematics and computational science at Duke Kunshan University and Duke University.
