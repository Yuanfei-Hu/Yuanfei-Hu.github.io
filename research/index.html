Research questions and themes

I am interested in how structured neural dynamics give rise to memory, representation and adaptive behavior, and how mechanistic models can connect observations across levels of analysis.

01

Neural dynamics and memory

How recurrent population dynamics maintain, transform or lose a representation—and how offline replay changes the relationship between memories.

Continuous attractors · neural fields · circular statistics

02

Adaptive computation

Which biologically grounded mechanisms—replay, salience, surprise and homeostatic regulation—can be formulated as testable principles for adaptive agents.

NeuroAI · intrinsic signals · adaptive agents

03

Geometry and system structure

How symmetry, invariance and dynamical consistency can constrain learning systems and make their failures easier to interpret.

Equivariance · anomaly detection · validation

Current questions

  1. How can offline neural dynamics reorganize recently formed representations?
  2. Which geometries allow neural and artificial systems to remain stable under transformation?
  3. How can computational models distinguish mechanistic explanation from descriptive fit?
